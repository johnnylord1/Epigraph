Component Hierarchy & Actions

Every element is tagged with its functional role. Tags: component action navigation state data-binding

widgets Shared Components

  • ▼ view_sidebar n-sidebar layout
    • n-sidebar__brand nav:home
    • n-sidebar-btn[Home] nav:route(/home)
    • n-sidebar-btn[Library] nav:route(/library)
    • n-sidebar-btn[Browse] nav:route(/browse)
    • n-sidebar-btn[Downloads] nav:route(/downloads)
    • n-sidebar-btn[Settings] nav:route(/settings)
  • ▼ web_asset n-topbar layout
    • n-search action:search(query)
    • n-segmented state:activeSection
    • n-toolbar-cluster container
  • ▼ call_to_action Buttons interactive
    • n-btn-primary action:submit
    • n-btn-secondary action:secondary
    • n-btn-ghost action:tertiary
    • n-icon-btn action:icon-action
  • ▼ filter_list n-popover state:openPanel
    • panel[density] state:gridDensity
    • panel[sort] state:sortMode,sortDir
    • panel[filter] state:filterValue
    • panel[layout] state:layoutMode

book_2 Library Screen

  • ▼ library-header
    • Segmented[Comics/Books] state:librarySection
    • n-chip[Reading] action:filterByStatus("READING")
    • n-chip[Completed] action:filterByStatus("COMPLETED")
    • n-chip[On Hold] action:filterByStatus("ON_HOLD")
    • n-chip[Plan to Read] action:filterByStatus("PLAN_TO_READ")
    • n-chip[Dropped] action:filterByStatus("DROPPED")
    • n-genre-chip[] action:filterByGenre(genre)
  • ▼ cover-grid data:library.items[]
    • n-library-card nav:route(/series/{id})
    • ├ cover image data:item.coverUrl
    • ├ progress-bar data:item.readProgress
    • ├ badge[NEW/CH] data:item.unreadCount
    • ├ title data:item.title
    • └ meta (author, vol) data:item.author, item.volume

auto_stories Series Detail

  • ▼ hero-section data:series
    • hero-backdrop data:series.coverUrl (blurred)
    • cover-image data:series.coverUrl
    • eyebrow data:series.type + series.source
    • title data:series.title
    • author data:series.author
    • genre-chips[] data:series.genres[]
    • stats (chapters/unread/rating) data:series.stats
  • ▼ action-bar
    • btn[Continue Ch. N] action:openReader(series.lastReadChapter+1)
    • btn[Bookmark] action:toggleInLibrary(series.id) state:isInLibrary
    • btn[Tracking] action:openTrackingSheet(series.id)
    • btn[Download] action:downloadAll(series.id)
    • btn[Migrate] action:openMigrateDialog(series.id)
  • ▼ chapter-controls
    • dropdown[Scanlator] state:selectedScanlator
    • btn[Sort] state:chapterSortMode
    • btn[Filter] state:chapterFilter
    • segmented[List/Grid] state:chapterViewMode
  • ▼ chapter-list data:series.chapters[]
    • chapter-row nav:openReader(chapter.id)
    • ├ title data:chapter.title
    • ├ progress data:chapter.readProgress
    • ├ scanlator data:chapter.scanlator
    • ├ date data:chapter.uploadDate
    • └ status (download/read) action:downloadChapter / markRead

chrome_reader_mode Reader

  • ▼ reader-top state:chromeVisible
    • btn[Back] nav:goBack()
    • series-title data:series.title
    • chapter-title data:chapter.title
    • btn[Bookmark] action:bookmarkPage(pageNum)
    • btn[Settings] action:openReaderSettings()
  • ▼ reader-canvas action:toggleChrome()
    • page-image data:pages[currentPage].url
    • error-overlay state:loadError
    • btn[Retry] action:retryPageLoad()
  • ▼ reader-bottom state:chromeVisible
    • page-slider state:currentPage action:goToPage(n)
    • btn[Prev/Next Page] action:prevPage/nextPage
    • mode-pill data:readingDirection
    • btn[FitWidth/FitHeight] state:fitMode
    • btn[ZoomIn/Out] action:zoom(delta)
    • btn[LTR/RTL/Vertical] state:readingDirection
  • reader-nav[Prev/Next Chapter] action:prevChapter/nextChapter
  • Keyboard: ←→ Space Esc action:navigate/toggleChrome

explore Browse

  • Segmented[Manga/Novels] state:browseContentType
  • SubTabs[Keyword/Browse/Source] state:browseTab
  • ▼ filter-pane
    • source-list state:selectedSource data:sources[]
    • genre-tags[] state:selectedGenres[] state:genreMode(AND/OR)
    • language-chips state:selectedLanguage
    • visibility-notice data:safetyFilter
  • browse-grid data:browseResults[]
  • btn[Load More] action:loadNextPage()

download Downloads & settings Settings

  • ▼ Downloads
    • status-summary data:queue.stats
    • btn[Pause All] action:pauseAllDownloads()
    • btn[Resume All] action:resumeAllDownloads()
    • dl-row[] data:queue.items[]
    • ├ btn[Pause] action:pauseDownload(id)
    • ├ btn[Resume] action:resumeDownload(id)
    • ├ btn[Retry] action:retryDownload(id)
    • └ btn[Remove] action:removeDownload(id)
  • ▼ Settings
    • category-sidebar state:settingsCategory
    • toggle[OLED/KeepScreen/AutoIndex] state:settings.*
    • slider[Accent/ScrollLimit] state:settings.*
    • segmented[CoverMode/ReadingMode/Quality] state:settings.*
    • btn[Import/Export Theme] action:importTheme/exportTheme
    • btn[Clean Cache] action:cleanCache()
    • integration-card[] action:connect/disconnect(service)
